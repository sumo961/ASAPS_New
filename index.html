Interactive digital narratives made with ASAPS

ASAPS is a visual tool for creating interactive digital narratives (IDNs). This is what one looks like when it is exported as a web page. It plays in your browser; nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere.

The Last Ferry

A night crossing in a storm. Pick who you are, back one of the crew in an argument, and see who stands by you when the ferry lurches. About three minutes; play it twice as different people.
